Account and Data Deletion

We value your privacy and give you full control over your data. If you no longer wish to use 4Spares and would like to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data, please follow the instructions below.

What happens when you delete your account?

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone number) will be permanently deleted.
  • Your order history and saved delivery addresses will be removed.
  • You will no longer be able to log in to the 4Spares application.
  • Any active orders will be cancelled.

Note: We may retain certain data for a limited period if required by law or for legitimate business purposes (such as fraud prevention or resolving disputes).

Request Deletion

To proceed with deleting your account, please send us an email from the address associated with your 4Spares account. Our support team will process your request within 7 business days.
